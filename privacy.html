Privacy Policy for My Cashbook

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: your cashbook lives on your device and, if you choose, in a Google Sheet inside your own Google account. We run no servers that receive your financial data, and we cannot see your Google Drive files.

1. Introduction

My Cashbook ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline-first personal financial application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Progressive Web App.

2. Data Collection and Storage

Local Storage: All transaction data, amounts, and remarks are initially stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. We do not transmit this data to our servers. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, local records that have not synced will be lost.

Device lock & biometrics: Your on-device data is stored in encrypted form. Unless you turn on a lock, the key is kept on the device so the app can open straight away; that hides the data from casual inspection but does not protect it from someone who can use your unlocked browser profile. If you add a master password and/or Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint (through your browser's passkey), the key is protected by them instead. Your password is never stored on the device, and the biometric option derives its key from your passkey, so no password is saved. If you forget your password or lose your passkey, you can erase this device's data and start again; entries already synced to your Google Sheet remain in your Sheet.

Sign-in token: When you sign in with Google, the app receives a short-lived access token (it expires in about one hour). It is kept in your browser session for this tab on your device so you do not have to sign in again immediately, and it is only ever sent to Google. Closing the tab discards the token.

Google Sheets Sync: If you choose to connect Google Sheets, your data is transmitted directly from your browser to Google's servers.

3. Google User Data and API Services

My Cashbook uses Google Sign-In (OAuth) so that it can create and update a spreadsheet in your Google Drive. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Scope Used: We request only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. It gives the app access only to files it creates or that you explicitly pick with Google Picker. It does not give access to the rest of your Google Drive.
  • Purpose: This scope is used strictly to create the cashbook spreadsheet, and to read and write that one spreadsheet (or the one you pick), so your cashbook entries can be backed up and shown in a dashboard.
  • Limited Use: We do not use Google user data for advertising, selling to third parties, or training machine learning models. We do not allow humans to read your data unless we have your affirmative consent or it is necessary for security purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google Identity Services, Google Picker, the Google Sheets API, and Google Fonts. Your use of these services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You retain full control over your data. You can delete local data by clearing your browser's site data. You can delete synced data by deleting the Google Spreadsheet created by this app in your Google Drive. You can withdraw the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/Olihemant24/offline-cashbook-pwa/issues.
