GET STARTED · ABOUT 1 MINUTE

Keep a backup in your own Google Sheet

My Cashbook already works offline on this device. Connect Google if you also want every entry saved in a spreadsheet that only you own, so you can see it on a computer and never lose your records.

  1. Choose your currency.
  2. Tap Create my cashbook Sheet and sign in with Google.
  3. Done. We set up the Sheet for you, and new entries sync after that.

▣ The app can only open the Sheet it creates (or one you pick). It cannot see your other Google Drive files, and your records never pass through our servers. Privacy Policy

About My Cashbook: My Cashbook is a privacy-first, offline-ready Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking personal finances. It stores your transaction data locally on your device and gives you the option to securely sync your cashbook to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. We do not collect or store your financial data on our servers.
